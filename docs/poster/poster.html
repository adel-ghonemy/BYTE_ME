BYTE.ME

Food, one byte away.

Pick a restaurant, fill your cart and order in a few taps. Then follow your order from pending to completed.

01 · WHYOrdering shouldn't be hard.

The problem المشكلة

Ordering by phone is slow

The line is busy, you can't see the menu or the prices, and you don't know when your food will come. Small restaurants lose orders because of it.

Our solution الحل

One menu, one cart, one tap

BYTE.ME shows each restaurant's dishes with photos and prices. You fill one cart, pay with cash, card or wallet, and follow your order in My orders.
